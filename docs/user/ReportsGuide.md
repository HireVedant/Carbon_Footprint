# EcoTrack Reports & Export Guide

## Exporting Your Carbon Report
EcoTrack allows you to export professional PDF reports summarizing your footprint and reduction strategy.

## Report Contents
- Executive Summary of annual footprint and Eco Score.
- Detailed emission breakdown by category.
- Scientific methodology and data source citations.
- Personalized reduction roadmap with top high-impact recommendations.

## How to Generate
1. Complete an assessment or open your Dashboard.
2. Click the **Export Report** button.
3. Your PDF will download automatically.
