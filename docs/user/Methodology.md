# EcoTrack Calculation Methodology

## Core Framework
EcoTrack AI follows the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol Corporate Standard for classifying greenhouse gas emissions:

- **Scope 1 (Direct Emissions):** Direct fuel combustion from personal vehicles and home heating (natural gas/heating oil).
- **Scope 2 (Indirect Energy Emissions):** Purchased electricity usage for home cooling, lighting, and appliances.
- **Scope 3 (Value Chain & Other Indirect):** Air travel, public transit, food production lifecycle emissions, waste decomposition, and goods/services consumption.

## Primary Emission Factor Sources
- **EPA Emission Factors Hub (2024):** US vehicle fuel economy standards, eGRID electricity emission factors, and fuel CO₂ contents.
- **IPCC Fifth Assessment Report (AR5):** Global Warming Potential (GWP) factors for CH₄ (methane) and N₂O (nitrous oxide).
- **DEFRA / BEIS Conversion Factors:** International flight distance matrices and indirect procurement emission multipliers.
