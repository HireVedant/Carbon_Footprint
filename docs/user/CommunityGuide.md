# EcoTrack Community & Leaderboard Guide

## Community Features
The EcoTrack Community dashboard displays real-time aggregated metrics from users worldwide.

## Leaderboards & Privacy
- **Leaderboard Ranking:** Top scores are ranked by Eco Score.
- **Anonymous Mode:** If you do not wish your name to appear on the leaderboard, toggle **Anonymous Mode** in your profile settings. Your score will still contribute to global stats as "Anonymous User".

## Global Aggregates
- **Total CO₂ Tracked:** Collective greenhouse gas emissions monitored on the platform.
- **Average Eco Score:** Platform-wide benchmark score.
