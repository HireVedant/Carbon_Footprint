# EcoTrack Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Is EcoTrack AI free?
A: Yes! Core carbon tracking, assessment tools, community access, and PDF reports are completely free.

### Q: How are calculations performed?
A: Calculations rely on greenhouse gas conversion factors published by the EPA, IPCC, and DEFRA.

### Q: Can I delete my data?
A: Yes. You can soft-delete or permanently purge your assessment history at any time from your account settings.

### Q: Is my financial or utility bill data shared?
A: Never. All calculations are privacy-first and processed locally or via secure Firebase backend functions.
