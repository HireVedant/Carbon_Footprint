# EcoTrack AI Features

EcoTrack AI is packed with tools to help you measure, manage, and mitigate your carbon emissions.

## 📊 Real-Time Carbon Simulator
Before even signing up, you can use our interactive simulator on the homepage. Adjust sliders for commuting, diet, and energy to instantly see how small lifestyle changes impact your overall carbon score.

## 📝 Comprehensive Assessments
- **Quick Assessment:** A rapid survey for a baseline estimate.
- **Detailed Assessment:** In-depth analysis of specific energy bills, vehicle MPG, dietary choices, and waste habits.

## 📈 Intelligent Dashboard
- **Visual Analytics:** Beautiful charts breaking down your emissions by category.
- **Historical Tracking:** Compare your current footprint against past months to track your reduction journey.

## 🌍 Community Leaderboards
Sustainability is a team effort.
- Compare your score against global averages.
- Opt-in to the leaderboard to compete with friends and other eco-conscious users.
- View anonymous global aggregates to see the platform's collective impact.

## 📑 Automated Reports
Export your data easily. EcoTrack generates professional PDF reports detailing your footprint, methodology used, and actionable reduction steps.

## 🔒 Privacy-First Design
Your data is encrypted and secure. We do not sell personal information to third parties. You have full control to delete your data or remain anonymous on the leaderboards.
