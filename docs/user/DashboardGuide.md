# EcoTrack Dashboard Guide

## Understanding Your Dashboard
Your dashboard is your central hub for carbon intelligence.

## Key Widgets
- **Total Annual Estimate (kg/tons CO₂e):** Your projected annual carbon footprint based on your latest assessment.
- **Eco Score (0–100):** A normalized score comparing your footprint to sustainable global benchmarks (higher score = lower environmental impact).
- **Category Breakdown Chart:** Interactive pie/bar chart showing emissions per category (Transport vs Energy vs Food).
- **Virtual Forest & Milestones:** Gamified progress tracking showing how many virtual trees your carbon savings represent.

## Setting Goals
You can set target reduction percentage goals (e.g. "Reduce footprint by 15% in 6 months") and monitor progress as you update your assessments.
