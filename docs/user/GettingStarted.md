# Getting Started with EcoTrack AI

Welcome to EcoTrack AI, your intelligent companion for carbon footprint tracking, reduction, and community engagement.

## What is EcoTrack AI?

EcoTrack AI is a modern, science-backed platform designed to help individuals and organizations understand and reduce their environmental impact. By analyzing your daily activities—such as transportation, energy usage, diet, and shopping habits—we calculate your carbon footprint and provide personalized, actionable insights.

## Creating an Account

1. Click on the **Get Started** button on the homepage.
2. Enter your email and a secure password, or sign in using your Google account.
3. Complete your basic profile.

## Your First Assessment

To get accurate insights, you need to complete a Carbon Assessment:

1. Navigate to the **Assessments** tab.
2. Choose either a **Quick Assessment** (takes 2 minutes) or a **Detailed Assessment** (takes 5-10 minutes).
3. Answer questions about your commuting habits, home energy, and diet.
4. Click **Calculate Impact**. 

You will instantly receive your Eco Score, total annual emissions, and personalized recommendations.

## Navigating the Dashboard

Once your assessment is complete, your dashboard will populate with real data:
- **Total Footprint**: Your annual CO₂ emissions.
- **Eco Score**: A rating from 0-100 indicating how sustainable your lifestyle is compared to the community.
- **Breakdown**: A visual chart showing exactly where your emissions come from (e.g., 40% Transport, 30% Energy).

## Community

Join the EcoTrack community to compare your progress:
- Visit the **Community** tab to see global statistics.
- Check the Leaderboard to see top eco-warriors.
- (Optional) Toggle your profile to anonymous if you prefer privacy.

We are excited to help you on your sustainability journey!
