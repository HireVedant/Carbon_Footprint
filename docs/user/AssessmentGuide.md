# EcoTrack Assessment Guide

## Overview
The EcoTrack Carbon Assessment measures your greenhouse gas emissions across 5 core areas of daily life.

## The 5 Pillars of Assessment
1. **Transport:** Commuting, public transit, personal vehicles, and flights.
2. **Energy:** Home electricity, heating, natural gas, and renewable energy offsets.
3. **Food & Diet:** Meat consumption, plant-based meals, and food waste.
4. **Waste & Recycling:** Municipal waste generation and recycling habits.
5. **Shopping & Consumption:** Clothing, electronics, and general purchasing.

## How to Get Accurate Results
- Have your monthly electric or gas bill handy.
- Estimate your average weekly vehicle driving hours or mileage.
- Be honest about dietary choices—even small adjustments matter.

## Saving & Reviewing
Once completed, your assessment results are saved securely to your account. You can revisit past assessments anytime to compare progress over time.
